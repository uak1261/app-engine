# Run prompts

## Claude Code: weekly scout

Read AGENTS.md, docs/engine.md, data/portfolio.json, and the last decision. Run this as the persistent engine, not the legacy /appkittie-brief. Inspect the live AppKittie tool schema and remaining credit budget. Discover and cluster narrowly defined iOS opportunities across the five lanes. Maintain stable IDs, at most 20 active ideas and five finalists. Spend no more than the weekly scout allocation, including all review and keyword charges. Save normalized evidence with source, scope, dates, and uncertainty. Recommend at most two validation experiments with preregistered success/kill/inconclusive rules. Run monthly-subscription scenarios through scripts/economics.py; flag other billing models for cohort-specific modeling. Do not invent CAC, retention, profitability, US revenue scope, or unavailable metrics. Separate facts, estimates, assumptions, and unknowns. Propose registry updates on a research branch and save reports/YYYY-MM-DD-scout.md. Give Codex a handoff listing evidence paths, assumptions, unresolved gates, and exact changes proposed. Make no advertising purchases or external submissions.

## Codex: independent audit

Read AGENTS.md, docs/engine.md, the latest scout, its normalized evidence, and the previous decision. Independently assess each finalist before reading Claude's final recommendation. Recompute economics, stress CPI by +50%, conversion by -30%, and variable costs by +100%, and show the consequence. Check gross/net and country/window consistency, independently owned comparators, sampled-review bias, billing cadence, actual founder hours, and the remaining six-month acquisition horizon. Require BOTH base LTV/CAC >=3 and payback <=3 months, positive downside unit contribution, and a funding plan before scale. Unknown budget means unresolved capital feasibility. State the strongest reason each idea fails. Preserve disagreements rather than averaging scores. Save reports/YYYY-MM-DD-audit.md on an audit branch. Do not spend duplicate research credits unless a specific missing fact warrants it. Recommend TEST, PRODUCT TEST, BUILD, HOLD, KILL, or SCALE with evidence appropriate to that stage.

## Daily monitor: either agent, one writer

Read the registry and last decision first. Use at most 30 AppKittie credits. Check material changes in the five finalists and active experiments. Reserve challenger search for an allocated day rather than restarting discovery. Save a dated pulse and propose updates with stable IDs. Report what changed, whether it changes a gate, and the single next action. If nothing meaningful changed, say so. Do not replace finalists without a documented promotion/removal decision. On tool failure, mark the run failed/partial and preserve state.

## Weekly decision: founder-led

Reconcile scout and audit evidence. Show up to five ideas and disagreements. Check capacity and known budgets. Choose at most two live validation experiments and one build. Append timestamped decisions with evidence paths, owner, rationale, and reversal conditions to data/portfolio.json. Set the next decision date. One designated writer commits registry changes after reconciliation; never let parallel branches silently overwrite the registry.
